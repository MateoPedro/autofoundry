# Auto Foundry

**A company that makes companies.**

Auto Foundry is an autonomous holding company built on the Naïve SDK. It finds demand, spawns a small agent-run company to serve it, measures whether that company can acquire paying customers, and kills it if it can't. The foundry learns from every death and casts the next one better.

No human picks the niches. No human runs the subsidiaries. People set the rules and the budget; the company does the rest.

## How it works

1. **Scout** continuously harvests demand signals: businesses with no website, stores with visible problems, people publicly asking for help. Signals are clustered and scored.
2. **CEO** spawns a company when a cluster is big enough and unserved. Each child is a Naïve config cloned from a template, with its own agents, inboxes, phone numbers, budget, and the leads already loaded.
3. **Children** acquire customers the only way a two-day-old company can: by delivering the finished thing first (a live website, a store audit, a translated listing) and charging a fixed price to keep it. Every child reports cost per touch and payments to the foundry.
4. **CEO** kills on CAC. A child that burns its budget without a paying customer is shut down and its lessons are logged. A cluster that converts gets a sibling.
5. **Compliance** enforces the rules file on every outbound action: B2B only, business hours only, AI disclosure in the first sentence, no residential numbers.

The only human approval gate is a new call script or new web copy. Everything else runs on `allow`.

## What's in this repo

- `naive.config.ts`: the foundry (Scout, CEO, Compliance)
- `templates/`: child company templates (local-site, shopify-fix, room-services)
- `companies/`: spawned children, generated by the CEO
- `spawn.ts`: clone a template, fill in niche and leads, run `naive up`
- `dashboard/`: lineage tree, per-company funnel, spend and revenue ledger

## Stack

Naïve (agents, identity, phone, budgets, approval gates), Supabase (shared board and ledger), Apify (demand scraping), Stripe (payments), Vercel (child sites and dashboard), OpenRouter (model routing: cheap for scouting, strong for fulfillment).
